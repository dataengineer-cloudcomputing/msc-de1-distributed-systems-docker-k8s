=== Self-healing demonstration ===

Command: kubectl delete pod flask-app-755c6bdf8-6ncvm -n msc-de1-project

Observed via: kubectl get pods -n msc-de1-project -w

Sequence:
1. flask-app-755c6bdf8-6ncvm -> Terminating
2. New pod flask-app-755c6bdf8-xfdz8 created automatically -> Pending -> ContainerCreating -> Running
3. Old pod -> Completed (removed)
4. Final state: 2/2 pods Running (desired replica count restored by the ReplicaSet)

Explanation: The Deployment's ReplicaSet continuously reconciles the actual
state with the desired state (replicas: 2). Deleting a pod reduces the
observed count below the desired count, triggering the ReplicaSet controller
to schedule a replacement pod automatically - no manual intervention needed.
