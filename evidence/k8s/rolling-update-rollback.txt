=== Rolling update & rollback demonstration ===

1. Added /version route (returns {"version": "1.1.0"}), built and pushed
   whale92400/msc-de1-flask-app:1.1.0 to Docker Hub.

2. Rolling update:
   Command: kubectl apply -f k8s/deployment.yaml (image bumped to 1.1.0)
   Command: kubectl rollout status deployment/flask-app -n msc-de1-project
   Result: "deployment flask-app successfully rolled out"
   Verified: both pods running 1.1.0, curl /version -> {"version":"1.1.0"}

3. Rollout history:
   REVISION 1 -> initial deploy (1.0.0)
   REVISION 2 -> update to 1.1.0

4. Rollback:
   Command: kubectl rollout undo deployment/flask-app -n msc-de1-project
   Result: "deployment.apps/flask-app rolled back"
   Verified: both pods back on whale92400/msc-de1-flask-app:1.0.0

Explanation: Deployments keep a revision history of ReplicaSets. A rolling
update creates a new ReplicaSet and gradually shifts traffic/pods from the
old one to the new one (maxUnavailable/maxSurge control the pace), keeping
the app available throughout. Rollback simply re-activates the previous
ReplicaSet's pod template, reversing the same mechanism.
